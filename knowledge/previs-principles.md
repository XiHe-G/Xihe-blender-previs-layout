# Previs Principles

Previs answers the director's spatial and temporal questions before detail is added. Establish the ground, dominant architecture, depth layers, cast identity, readable silhouettes, action beats, and camera intent in that order. Every shot should make the next decision easier: staging, timing, lens, cut, or continuity.

Use the least geometry that preserves scale, occlusion, direction, and performance. A colored proxy, a face marker, and a clear target can carry more review value than a detailed model that hides the blocking problem.
