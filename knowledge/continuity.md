# Continuity

Carry forward actor positions, facing, pose/state, screen direction, and the viewer's understanding of the dominant landmark. A cut can change lens or distance, but it should not silently swap left/right relationships or erase the reason for the next action.

V1 records one self-contained plan per shot and emits a sequence movie. Use the debug movie to inspect beat labels and frame boundaries; use the contact sheet to compare beginning, middle, and end composition quickly.
