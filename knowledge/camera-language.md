# Camera Language

Choose a shot size and movement that preserve the beat. `STATIC` observes, `PAN` or `TILT` redirects attention, `FOLLOW` preserves a moving subject, `ARC` reveals spatial relations, and `HANDHELD_APPROX` adds restrained instability. Supply explicit start/end locations and targets whenever composition matters.

Use lens and height to control scale and hierarchy. A camera move should reveal, follow, or hand off attention; do not move merely because the shot is long. For a multi-shot sequence, each shot has its own camera plan and the assembler concatenates the review movies.
