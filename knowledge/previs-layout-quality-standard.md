# PREVIS_LAYOUT_QUALITY_REFERENCE_V1

The archived 30-second video is a historical reference for layout readability. A compact visual index is preserved at `../assets/PREVIS_LAYOUT_QUALITY_REFERENCE_V1-contact-sheet.jpg`. Use a current project's supplied quality reference when available; the historical video is not a required attachment or a source of project blocking. Its reusable observable properties are:

- A simple grey environment still communicates ground, bridge/cliff/rail, foreground, middle distance, and background.
- Characters remain visually distinct through saturated proxy colors, stable silhouettes, head/face direction markers, and consistent scale.
- Wide, medium, and closer views preserve screen-space relationships; camera motion has a reason and does not erase the staging.
- Cuts or changes of view are motivated by the action beat. The viewer can identify who acts, who reacts, and what remains held.
- The result is a reviewable blocking movie, not a final animation render.

V1 passes when a first-time viewer can answer who is where, what changed, where the camera is looking, and whether the next shot inherits the intended state. Fine materials, facial animation, cloth, and effects are outside this quality gate.

For current COMBAT_DYNAMIC work, use `../references/combat-dynamic-motion.md` in addition to this historical baseline: semantic ACTION/WEAPON/VFX proxies are part of action readability, and ACTION_READABILITY_GATE plus SILENT_REVIEW apply. Final polished effects remain outside scope; NARRATIVE keeps its existing criteria.
