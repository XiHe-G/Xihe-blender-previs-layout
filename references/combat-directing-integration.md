# Combat directing integration

Preserve threat → perception → response → consequence across actors and cuts. Each beat needs a visible cause, an observable effect and enough screen time to read the change. A source/video study can inform these decisions but cannot override a confirmed storyboard.

Balance short explosive windows with preparation and recovery. Author route distance and time before decorative effects. Compare world displacement with subject scale, tracking and environmental flow; rooted body response alone cannot establish a collision consequence.

Historical project evidence and director discussions are not included in the public package. No prior technical PASS establishes automatic full-fight choreography or director approval.
