# Narrative default and explicit variants

Default NARRATIVE_BOX_5 follows Image 2 in `layout-presentation-standard.md`: sphere head, horizontal eye bar, cone nose, one body cuboid representing torso and limbs, chest-facing bar. Five meshes, no arms/hands/separate legs/feet. Keep identity color, cast proportions, bottom-center root, body and independent head control. Eyes and nose follow HEAD_CTRL; chest marker follows BODY.

Narrative acting prioritizes eyelines, orientation, readable intent, pauses, approach/retreat and group relationships. Do not replace a listening hold with constant rocking. Movement follows terrain and approved routes, with intentional start/stop and head/body changes.

Only explicit user/model requirements enable articulated or different head/body variants. Props can remain if they communicate a real event and are not excluded; no automatic limbs to hold them. Recompose a hand-detail shot into its approved body/prop relationship when that is the chosen abstraction, and disclose the lost detail. A/B production is optional. If requested, keep a shared story, edit timeline and route plan; use separate .blend/visibility/render outputs and document camera exceptions.

This prepares reference media for downstream generation. Do not assert better Seedance output without an actual matched generation comparison. Final model prompting is a separate task. Fights, running, jumps and force-driven sequences use COMBAT_DYNAMIC; mixed sequences retain an approved consistent character construction across cuts.
